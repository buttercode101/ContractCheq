# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Our Pledge
- We will treat everyone with respect, dignity, and fairness.
- Harassment, discrimination, and intimidation are not tolerated.
- We expect participants to be friendly and respectful to each other.

## Expected Behavior
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Ask for permission before referencing someone else's work.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or threats.
- Hate speech or insults based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any protected characteristic.
- Unwelcome sexual advances or other inappropriate interpersonal behavior.

## Reporting Issues
If you experience or witness any violations of this Code of Conduct, please contact:
- Email: **security@buttercode101.com**
- Provide a clear description of the incident, parties involved, and any evidence.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
