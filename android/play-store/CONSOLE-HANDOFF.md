# Play Console handoff — new personal account

1. Complete the owner's identity/contact/device verification shown by Play Console. Do not publish personal contact information by inference.
2. Confirm each permanent application ID and developer-owned upload signing key. Enable Play App Signing through the owner's controlled console flow; back up upload credentials privately.
3. Supply actual support email, publisher identity, policy URLs, target audience, countries and pricing. Review listing-draft.json and Data Safety engineering notes against the release binary and providers.
4. Upload a correctly signed release AAB to internal testing. Debug APKs and unsigned AABs are not production submissions.
5. Obtain real screenshots and, for ScreenNote, the foreground-service use-case demonstration. Complete applicable content-rating, Data Safety, ads, app access, financial features, billing and foreground-service forms accurately.
6. Set up a closed test for EACH app: at least 12 opted-in testers continuously for 14 days for this newly-created personal account, then apply for production access. Periods may overlap; do not invent feedback/tester participation. Internal testing does not satisfy the closed-test requirement.
7. Review tester feedback, pre-launch reports, crashes/ANRs and accessibility; fix/retest. Apply for production access and submit only after the owner reviews the concrete release.
