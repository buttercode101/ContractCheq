# Phase 3 launch brief

## Positioning
Understand what you're signing before you sign it. ContractCheq is plain-language contract risk triage, not legal advice.

## Initial wedge
Residential leases first: deposits, cancellation/notice, inspections/entry, one-sided fees, privacy/data clauses. Secondary: employment contracts. Expand only from observed use.

## ICP
Primary user/buyer: SA tenant about to sign/renew/cancel a residential lease. Trigger: contract in hand and uncertainty about a clause. Alternative: Google/Reddit/friend/lawyer. Trust barrier: legal accuracy and document privacy.

## Activation
Upload a real contract -> extraction succeeds -> user opens at least one finding/explanation.

## North star
Completed contract checks that reach at least one reviewed finding.

## Pricing experiment
Keep free preview. Current R19 single check is a low-friction transaction test; compare conversion against R39 pack-of-3 equivalent and existing R99 pack-of-10 economics before raising price. Do not claim legal certainty.

## Experiments
1. Lease-intent SEO resource -> lease demo/upload. Success: completed analyses, not visits. 14 days.
2. Helpful answers in tenant/job-seeker communities only where self-promotion is allowed -> relevant demo. Success: qualified activations. 14 days.
3. Pricing page -> checkout-start -> verified payment. Success: real paid conversion. 30 days.

## Launch copy
**One line:** Before you sign a lease or employment contract, see the clauses worth a second look.

**Community-safe post:** I built a South African contract triage tool after seeing how often people only discover a bad clause after signing. It gives a plain-language first pass and shows why a clause deserves attention; it does not replace a lawyer. I'm looking specifically for people with leases or employment contracts who can tell me where the explanation is confusing or untrustworthy.
